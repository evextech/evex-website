WHAT CAN GO WRONG

Monitor the mechanisms that matter.

Monitoring should be selected around the physical degradation and failure mechanisms relevant to the asset and its operating environment.

  • Insulation deterioration
  • Partial discharge and internal arcing
  • Thermal abnormalities and cooling issues
  • Load tap changer (LTC) deterioration
  • Bushing and connection deterioration
  • Mechanical looseness, vibration, or winding/core changes

SIGNALS THAT MATTER

Use measurements that relate to the problem.

No single signal provides complete visibility. The useful combination depends on the failure mechanism, environment, and information already available.

TemperatureThermal response and hotspot indicators
LoadingOperating context and load response
DGADissolved-gas trends where available
Acoustic / UltrasonicPhysical signatures associated with abnormal activity
Partial DischargeElectrical-discharge indicators
Moisture / OilSupporting condition information
ElectricalVoltage, current, harmonics and related measurements
EnvironmentAmbient conditions affecting interpretation

EVEX APPROACH

Multi-signal change detection.

Every transformer operates differently.
