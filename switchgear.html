WHAT CAN GO WRONG

Monitor the mechanisms that matter.

Monitoring should be selected around the physical degradation and failure mechanisms relevant to the asset and its operating environment.

  • Insulation degradation
  • Partial-discharge activity
  • Hot joints and connection problems
  • Humidity, condensation, or contamination effects
  • Breaker or contact deterioration

SIGNALS THAT MATTER

Use measurements that relate to the problem.

No single signal provides complete visibility. The useful combination depends on the failure mechanism, environment, and information already available.

Partial DischargeUHF, TEV, ultrasound and related indicators
TemperatureBusbar, termination, breaker and contact behavior
HumidityEnvironmental context for insulation risk
Current / LoadOperating context for interpretation
UltrasoundAcoustic indicators of abnormal electrical activity
EnvironmentCondensation, salt, dust and temperature cycling
Passive SensingBattery-free / wireless options where practical
Trend HistoryChange over time rather than one-time detection

MECHANISM INTELLIGENCE

Circuit Breaker Mechanism Intelligence

Where available, breaker-operating signals add a mechanical dimension to electrical and thermal monitoring. Signals are technology-agnostic and not assumed to exist on every installation.

EVEX APPROACH

Asset-first and technology-agnostic.

Start with existing operational information and the diagnostic objective. Add sensing only where it creates meaningful visibility, then use engineering analytics, trend analysis, asset-specific baselines, anomaly detection, and sensor fusion where appropriate.

TYPICAL ENGAGEMENT
